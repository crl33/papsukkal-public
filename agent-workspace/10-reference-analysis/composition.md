## The red sprays — the most-missed detail

They are **not** one blossom per stalk. Each is a branching corymb: several
fine stalks leaving a stem, each carrying a tight umbel of 15–30 tiny scarlet
blossoms, packed densely enough that the clusters read as an airy cloud and the
stems nearly vanish behind them. "Sticks with a red dot on top" is the failure
mode, and it has recurred twice.

## Background

Defocused poppies (ring + dark heart) upper right around 0.68–0.97, 0.02–0.15;
dark maroon blooms upper left; a luminous cyan/azure "sky gap" upper
centre-right (brightest ≈0.58, 0.06); everything else a deep navy-teal wash
darkening into the corners.

## Density

Visual density is high but the *sharp* population is small: 8–10 readable
flowers. Everything else is soft. The eye should struggle to count the plants.
